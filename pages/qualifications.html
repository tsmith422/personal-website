After demonstrating an aptitude for programming in my first Java course, my teacher encouraged me to join the Computer Science team. That year, our team placed first in our region and second in the state, while I individually placed 13th statewide.

I also competed in Calculator Applications, where I placed first in my district and fifth in my region. Although I narrowly missed qualifying for the state competition, the experience challenged me to strengthen my mathematical and problem-solving skills. Competing in UIL was an especially rewarding experience that helped develop my interest in programming and reinforced my enjoyment of challenging technical problems.
