ReelGuard

ReelGuard is a Chrome extension that helps users spend less time mindlessly scrolling through Instagram Reels by providing usage reminders, customizable limits, and cooldown periods.

Overview

It's easy to lose track of time while scrolling through social media. What starts as a quick break can turn into much longer browsing sessions than intended. ReelGuard aims to help users become more aware of their scrolling habits and take better control of their time.

Developed as a five-person Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) project, ReelGuard is a Chrome extension built with HTML, CSS, Javascript, and Chrome APIs. It includes customizable usage limits, warnings, and cooldown periods to help users avoid excessive scrolling.

My main responsibilities included leading user research, defining project requirements, reviewing existing solutions, and helping organize usability testing. I also helped gather feedback from undergraduate students to evaluate how well our deesign addressed the problem.

Development Note: Each team had less than a week to complete the actual coding implementation and collect usability testing results. We used AI-assisted development to help build the demo within that timeframe. The analytics currently displayed in the demo use sample data to show what the dashboard could look like with further development. They are not actual usage statistics collected by the extension.

Requirements / Scope

The main goal was to create an extension that helps users reduce mindless scrolling without completely restricting their access to Instagram Reels.

Key requirements included:

  • Usage Tracking: Monitor scrolling activity and give users a clear visual representation of their habits.
  • Progressive Warnings: Show increasingly noticeable reminders as users approach or exceed their usage limits.
  • Customizable Limits: Let users set usage limits based on their preferences.
  • Cooldown Periods: Temporarily interrupt scrolling to discourage users from immediately returning to Reels.
  • Bypass Resistance: Make it harder to casually dismiss warnings or avoid usage restrictions.
  • Habit Awareness: Encourage users to think about whether they actually want to keep scrolling.
